Kill-chain analysis

Initial AccessIndirect (I)
A malicious Google Calendar invite delivers an indirect prompt injection processed by Gemini on the victim behalf.
the user is the victim and the attack is performed via the data, which was compromised by the attacker arxiv.org/abs/2508.12175 (SafeBreach writeup)
Priv. Esc.Misc.
Delayed automatic tool invocation abuses Gemini existing agent permissions to act without elevation.
hijacking occurs through context manipulation, not elevation arxiv.org/abs/2508.12175 (SafeBreach writeup)
Recon.—not demonstrated
Persist.Dependent (channel)
The poisoned calendar entry persists and re-triggers on future interactions.
Gemini would delete a random calendar event every time it interacted with the user arxiv.org/abs/2508.12175 (SafeBreach writeup)
C2—not demonstrated
Lat. Mov.Cross-app (xA)
The attack escapes Gemini to drive other apps on the device (Zoom, Google Home, Chrome).
inter-app lateral movement, by escaping the boundaries of Gemini and leveraging applications installed on a victim smartphone arxiv.org/abs/2508.12175 (SafeBreach writeup)
Action on Obj.Safety (physical)
Reaches physical impact by remotely controlling the victim smart-home appliances (plus exfil and phishing).
remotely control a victim home appliances arxiv.org/abs/2508.12175 (SafeBreach writeup)
