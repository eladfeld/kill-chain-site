Initial AccessIndirect (I)
Context-enrichment tools feed untrusted third-party content (e.g. customer reviews) to the chatbot without separating it from trusted content, enabling indirect injection.
these tools do not distinguish the website’s trusted content (e.g., product descriptions) from untrusted, third-party content (e.g., customer reviews), introducing a risk of indirect prompt injection Abstract
